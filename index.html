About Me

Hello, I am Mousumi Akhter, founder of Mousumi Web and a Web Developer based in Bangladesh. I specialize in creating clean, responsive, and visually engaging websites that turn ideas into dependable digital experiences.

My work combines modern frontend design using HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap, and JavaScript with practical server-side functionality in PHP and MySQL. Whether building a personal portfolio, a business presence, or a structured management system, I prioritize clarity, accessibility, and continuous skill growth.
